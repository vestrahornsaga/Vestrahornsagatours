Practical information

Meeting point

Viking Cafe, Horni, 781 Höfn.

Group size

Small groups of 2 to 10 people. Travelling solo? Send a request anyway and we'll try to match you with a group (tours run with a minimum of 2 people). Private group prices on request.

Languages

Guided in English, Spanish or Catalan.

Payment

Pay on the day at the Viking Cafe, card accepted. No prepayment needed.

What to bring

Hiking boots, waterproof jacket and trousers, warm layers, hat and gloves, water and snacks. In winter, bring your own microspikes: they are not provided.

Weather plan

Safety comes first. If the mountain is unsafe, we switch to the Stokksnes Saga Tour and you pay the lower price. If no tour is possible, we cancel at no cost to you.

Frequently asked questions

Which tour should I choose?

Choose the Vestrahorn Saga Tour for the complete experience: the Stokksnes walk plus a full day of hiking on the mountain. Choose the Stokksnes Saga Tour for the same walk and evening without the big hike, or if you are short on time.

Do I need hiking experience?

Not for the Stokksnes Saga Tour. The Vestrahorn Saga Tour needs good general fitness, but no climbing experience.

Is the access ticket included?

Yes. The Vestrahorn access ticket, dinner and one drink per person are included in both tours. Extra drinks can be bought on site.

Will we see the northern lights?

They can appear on clear, dark nights from autumn to early spring, but they are never guaranteed.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about allergies or preferences when you book.

How do I book and pay?

Send us a message with your tour, date and number of people. We confirm availability, send a reminder 48 hours before, and you pay on the day at the Viking Cafe.
